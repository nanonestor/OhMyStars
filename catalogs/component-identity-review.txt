OMS COMPONENT IDENTITY CONFLICTS - EDITABLE RESEARCH AND DECISIONS
Audit date: 2026-10-21 (local time)

PURPOSE
Iota Cassiopeiae B disappeared because a repeated system-level WDS DM identifier
was interpreted as that component's own HD identity. The linked AT-HYG row
already represented Aa, so identifier enrichment dropped B and its relationships.
Iota Cas B has been corrected separately; it is NOT one of the open cases below.

This audit found 59 further omission events across 58 WDS systems:
47 DM-to-HD conflicts and 12 HDS-to-HIP conflicts. These are candidates for
research, NOT 59 proven missing physical companions. Some may be valid duplicate
identifications or cross-system aliases. Removing one disputed endpoint also
removes its incident pair measurements and can remove other, orphaned components
or an entire system. Research the complete local hierarchy, not just the label.

WHAT TO EDIT
For each entry, fill in Decision, Evidence, and Notes. Suggested decisions:
  PENDING - not researched.
  RESTORE - distinct star; retain its WDS identity/measurements and reject the
            misleading identifier link. Specify C, V, Q, or U only with evidence.
  KEEP OMITTED - genuine duplicate; name the retained OMS/AT-HYG identity.
  REASSIGN - wrong match or label; give the correct identities and source pairs.
  NEEDS MORE EVIDENCE - unresolved; do not change catalog behavior yet.
A decision should distinguish identity, component label, and physical membership.
Not being part of a physical system does not mean an optical V entry is deleted.
Include URLs, publication references, source rows, and any measured separations
or magnitudes supporting your conclusion. Do not invent missing measurements.

HOW TO CONTINUE LATER
Ask the coding assistant to read this file and apply ONLY entries with explicit
decisions, preserving unresolved entries. This file is not automatically executed
or regenerated. Do not make corrections only in oms_catalog.csv: implement them
in tools\CatalogBuilder so the next regeneration preserves them.
Relevant code:
  tools\CatalogBuilder\Identifiers.cs: EnrichIdentifiers and
      OmitDuplicateRelationships (the identifier enrichment/deletion stage).
  tools\CatalogBuilder\IdentityMatching.cs: initial component identification.
  tools\CatalogBuilder\Program.cs: curated roles, identity checks and manifest.
  tools\CatalogBuilder\Validation.cs and CatalogTests.cs: source/role checks.
  tools\MultiTests\Program.cs: real-system identities and plot geometry.
Document accepted special cases in catalogs\oms-notes.txt and add regression
tests. Preserve known AT-HYG fields, original WDS pair measurements, and reserved
OMS IDs in identity-registry.csv. A primary/wide-pair identifier may identify a
whole unresolved subsystem; do not assign it to every leaf.
Run CatalogBuilder --self-test, regenerate with catalogs\Generate.ps1, and
validate the regenerated catalog. Verify affected Multi layouts. The real-catalog
Multi run currently has an unrelated Albireo-name lookup failure after orbit
checks; distinguish it from new failures. Deploy only when the game releases its
DLL lock and preserve installed settings.

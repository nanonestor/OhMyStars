2. catalogs/oms_catalog.csv

The compiled OMS catalog is a modified and supplemented AT-HYG 4.0 catalog
from the HYG Star Database (David Nash).
URL: https://astronexus.com/projects/at-hyg

This file is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
See LICENSES/CC_BY-SA_4.0.txt for the full license text.

Copyright © David Nash.

Original nonempty AT-HYG fields are preserved; additional identifiers and
individual-star candidates use the Washington Double Star Catalog maintained
at the U.S. Naval Observatory. Supported ORB6 matches supply additional
component classifications. Sources, hashes, attribution and limitations are
documented in catalogs/attributions.md and catalogs/oms_catalog_readme.md.
Input hashes and construction details remain in the source repository's
catalogs/build-manifest.json and catalogs/README.md.
The older reduced AT-HYG 3.2 CSV remains historical repository material but
is not included in the mod build.

4. catalogs/orb6orbits.txt and catalogs/orb6format.txt

Sixth Catalog of Orbits of Visual Binary Stars (ORB6).
Rachel A. Matson, Stephen J. Williams, William I. Hartkopf and Brian D. Mason,
U.S. Naval Observatory.
URL: https://www.astro.gsu.edu/wds/orb6.html
Citation: Hartkopf, Mason and Worley (2001), Sixth Catalog of Orbits of
Visual Binary Stars, and the individual orbit references in the catalog.

This research has made use of the Washington Double Star Catalog maintained
at the U.S. Naval Observatory.

The downloaded orbital-elements and format files are retained unmodified.
Oh My Stars derives apparent relative orbit curves from supported records.
Snapshot URLs, retrieval date and hashes are in catalogs/orb6-source.json;
interpretation and limitations are documented in catalogs/oms_catalog_readme.md
and the supplied catalogs/orb6format.txt.
